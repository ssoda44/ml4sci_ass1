## Exploratory data analysis (Ground Truth only)

The four panels reuse saved Ground Truth histograms from 2,000 held-out scenes, covering frames 10–29; they are descriptive summaries, not training-set statistics used to choose preprocessing or hyperparameters. No model predictions are included.

Each panel reports the fraction outside its displayed range. Tortuosity is shown over 0–5, with about 44% outside this range; large values can reflect small net displacement rather than turning alone. Its denominator is floored at 0.01 m. Densities retain the full-population denominator, so the displayed area may be below one.
